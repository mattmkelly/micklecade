Pick a paper, canvas, board or wall, then pick a paint on the left: watercolor, oil, acrylic, spray paint, markers or oil crayon. Pick a color on the right and paint with your finger or the mouse. Watercolor flows and dries by itself -- paint into wet paint for soft blooms, or try a WASH. Oil stays wet so colors mix; acrylic dries fast. Hold spray paint still to make drips. Crayon resists watercolor! UNDO takes a step back, DRY dries everything, SAVE keeps your picture.
