HOW TO PLAY

STEER

Your kart drives all by itself. You just steer!

KEYS ← →PAD STICKTOUCH STICK / HOLD A SIDE
HOP & DRIFT

Hop over things. Hold HOP round a bend to make sparks, let go to ZOOM!

KEYS SPACEPAD ATOUCH GREEN ARROW
ITEM BOXES

Drive through a box. Carrots zoom, snowballs and honey bonk the others.

KEYS ↑PAD XTOUCH ITEM
BRAKE

Slow down for tight bends. Keep holding to back up.

KEYS ↓PAD BTOUCH STOP SIGN
JUMPS & TRICKS

Press HOP just before the top of a jump for a big floaty trick and a boost!

COINS

Grab koala coins for new hats and kart paint. Win the Grand Prix for the GOLDEN kart!

RACEPick a track. Race 3 laps against the others.
GRAND PRIXAll the tracks in a row. Most points wins the cup!
BATTLESPop the most balloons, or stack up the most coins, in 2 minutes.
BUILD A TRACKDraw your own track, add jumps, portals and walls, then race it.
Two players? Player 1 uses A D, S, SPACE, W and player 2 the ARROWS, RIGHT SHIFT, UP (or a controller each).
Little drivers: set a gentle stick and AUTO-STEER in the ☰ menu. Stuck or going the wrong way? Puff the cloud lifts you back.
