HOW TO PLAY

🎼
METRONOME

Pick a speed with the slider, the + and − buttons, or slide the weight. Or tap TAP along to a song!

👂
NAME THAT TEMPO

Listen to the beat. Is it Adagio? Allegro? Presto? Pick the right music word.

🥁
HIT THE NUMBER

You get a number like 96. Tap the drum at exactly that speed!

BPM means beats per minute: 60 BPM is one beat every second. Tap 🔊 to hear how to say a tempo word. VOICE: OFF stops it reading words out when you tap the tempo ladder.
