COZY COVE

BUILD YOUR OWN LITTLE ISLAND TOWN

TAP TO START

PICK A TOOL, THEN TAP OR DRAG ON THE ISLAND. LAND GROWS THE ISLAND. USE LAND ON LAND TO RAISE A HILL. MOUNTAIN GROWS A PEAK -- HOLD IT TO KEEP GROWING. RIVERS POUR OVER EDGES AS WATERFALLS. A PATH ACROSS A RIVER MAKES A BRIDGE. BEACH MAKES SAND -- TAP IT AGAIN TO DECORATE. HOUSES NEXT TO EACH OTHER JOIN UP. PATHS MAKE STEPS UP HILLS. TAP A HOUSE AGAIN TO BUILD IT TALLER -- HOLD IT TO KEEP STACKING. EVERY FLOOR BRINGS A NEW NEIGHBOR. TAP A TREE, PATH, DOCK OR SHOP AGAIN TO CHANGE IT. A DOCK BETWEEN TWO SHORES BECOMES A BRIDGE. TAP A VILLAGER TO SAY HI. MY ISLANDS (TOP LEFT) HOLDS EVERY ISLAND -- THEY SAVE BY THEMSELVES. MAKE, RENAME, COPY OR DELETE THEM THERE. PHOTO TAKES A PICTURE YOU CAN SAVE TO THE DEVICE. STYLE IS UP HERE IN THE MENU. EVERY GROUP OF 3 OR MORE HOMES IS A TOWN. TAP A TOWN'S NAME TO RENAME IT. WALK (TOP RIGHT), THEN TAP A SPOT, PUTS YOU THERE ON YOUR ISLAND, SEEING THROUGH A VILLAGER'S EYES: LEFT THUMB MOVES, RIGHT THUMB LOOKS ROUND (OR ARROWS TO WALK AND TURN, A / D TO STEP SIDEWAYS), JUMP OR SPACE, BUILD TO GO BACK. HOLIDAYS COME BY THEMSELVES -- OR PICK ONE WITH HOLIDAY IN THE MENU. KEEP AN EYE ON THE SEA, DAY AND NIGHT... TWO FINGERS: PINCH, TWIST AND SLIDE THE VIEW. KEYS: ARROWS + SPACE, 1-9 AND 0 TOOLS, Q/E TURN, C COLOR, N NIGHT, M MUSIC, I ISLANDS, G WALK, Z UNDO. CONTROLLER: A BUILD, B DIG, LB/RB TOOL, X COLOR, Y NIGHT.
