COZY COVE

BUILD YOUR OWN LITTLE ISLAND TOWN

TAP TO START

PICK A TOOL, THEN TAP OR DRAG ON THE ISLAND. LAND GROWS THE ISLAND. USE LAND ON LAND TO RAISE A HILL. HOUSES NEXT TO EACH OTHER JOIN UP. PATHS MAKE STEPS UP HILLS. TAP A HOUSE AGAIN TO BUILD IT TALLER. EVERY FLOOR BRINGS A NEW NEIGHBOR. TAP A TREE, PATH, DOCK OR SHOP AGAIN TO CHANGE IT. A DOCK BETWEEN TWO SHORES BECOMES A BRIDGE. TAP A VILLAGER TO SAY HI. STYLE AND ISLAND 1-3 ARE UP HERE IN THE MENU. TWO FINGERS: PINCH, TWIST AND SLIDE THE VIEW. KEYS: ARROWS + SPACE, 1-7 TOOLS, Q/E TURN, C COLOR, N NIGHT, M MUSIC, Z UNDO. CONTROLLER: A BUILD, B DIG, LB/RB TOOL, X COLOR, Y NIGHT.
