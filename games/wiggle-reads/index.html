HOW TO PLAY

Read with Wiggle the bookworm! Pick your grade, then pick a way to play.

🗺️
TRAIL

Walk Wiggle along a trail of 12 stops. Each stop is a short reading round. 📖 stops are little stories with questions. Finish a stop to open the next one and win up to 3 stars.

🎮
ARCADE

Quick word games against the clock: pop the right balloon, catch the fireflies that fit the rule, and hook word cars onto the train in the right order.

🌻
GARDEN

Answer questions to earn seeds 🌱, then spend them on flowers, trees, animals and houses for Wiggle's garden. Stars and arcade points earn seeds too!

🔊 Listen: tap the speaker to hear it again. Wiggle reads out loud if your device has a voice.
🤔 Oops is OK: a wrong answer just wiggles and gives a hint. Try again! Stars count first tries.
⌨️ Keys: arrows move, Enter picks, 1-4 choose an answer, S says it again, Esc goes back.
🎮 Controller: D-pad moves, A picks, B goes back, Y says it again.
