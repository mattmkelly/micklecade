HOW TO PLAY

1. LEARN

Professor Newt shows you how things move.

2. GUESS

What will happen? Pick, then watch!

3. PUZZLES

Place ramps, fans and more. Press GO!

  • Drag a piece up from the tray. Tap ⟲ ⟳ to turn it. Drag it back down to put it away.
  • Get the ball into the green basket. Grab the stars for more points!
  • Stuck? Tap 💡 for a hint. Red dots show where the ball went last time.
  • Keyboard / controller: arrows move the hand, Enter / A grabs, Q E / LB RB turn, Space / Y is GO.
